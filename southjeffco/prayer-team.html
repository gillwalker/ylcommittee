South Jeffco · Committee Role

Prayer Team

Collects prayer requests from the local area staff and volunteers and prays together on a regular basis.

← Back to South Jeffco Roles

Overview

Being a Young Life staff person or a volunteer leader is a rewarding responsibility. However, building friendships with kids in order to earn the right to tell them about Jesus makes staff and leaders more susceptible to spiritual warfare. Therefore, staff and leaders need extra care and support. The local committee can have a major role in providing this care through prayer.

⏰ Time Requirement: 8 hours per month

Job Description

  • On a consistent basis, collect prayer requests from the local area staff and distribute those requests to several different groups.

Basic Strategies to Develop

Develop three layers of prayer strategy for the area:

  • Area-Wide Prayer Strategy: Involve as many people as possible from inside and outside the area who will pray for the Young Life ministry. Obtain general prayer requests from the area director monthly. Develop an e-mail distribution list drawing from committee, donors, church prayer groups, volunteer leaders, staff, banquet list, and others.
  • Committee-Wide Prayer Strategy: The committee should pray as a group for staff, volunteer leaders, and donors about key issues related to the ministry. Make prayer an important part of the committee meeting — consider an hour of prayer before the meeting or a weekly lunch gathering.
  • Individual Prayer Strategy: Each committee member should be paired with a specific staff member or volunteer leader to pray specifically for them. The committee member solicits individual prayer requests from their assigned person.

Qualifications

  • Understands the power of prayer.
  • Has the gift of prayer.
  • Is able to elicit the needs and concerns of others.
  • Good listening skills.
  • Can organize.
