Ministry Admin Support

Helps the area director stay on top of the administrative side of the ministry, so more of their time goes to kids, leaders and the South Jeffco community.

← Back to South Jeffco Roles

Overview

Our area director carries a lot: building relationships with kids across South Jeffco, training and caring for volunteer leaders, meeting with donors, partnering with the committee and reporting to the region. Behind all of that is a steady stream of calendars, deadlines, reports and updates that can quietly pull time and energy away from ministry.

The Ministry Admin Support volunteer is a committee member who comes alongside the area director to lighten that load. Working mostly from home, on your own schedule, you help keep the calendar current, projects moving and communication flowing, often without anyone noticing, and that is exactly the point. Few roles free up more of an area director's time for what matters most.

Time commitment: 2–3 hours per week, almost entirely remote.

Job Description

  • Manage the calendar. Help maintain the area director's calendar and the area ministry calendar (club, Campaigners, camp, committee meetings and events), and help schedule meetings with donors, leaders and committee members.
  • Keep projects on schedule. Track key deadlines and recurring projects, such as banquet prep, fall kickoff, camp and the annual plan, and give friendly nudges so nothing slips.
  • Support regional reporting. Help gather and submit the reports and information the regional office requests, on time.
  • Send regular communication. Draft and send updates to leaders, parents, committee members and donors on behalf of the area director, using the communication tools Young Life provides.
  • Find new ways to help. As you learn the rhythms of the area, look for other places where you can take something off the area director's plate.

No one is expected to do all of this. The role is shaped around what helps our area director most and what fits the time you have.

Examples of Where You Might Help

  • Tracking camp dates, registration deadlines, scholarship forms and payment reminders.
  • Keeping a shared task list with the area director and following up on action items.
  • Taking notes at committee meetings and following up on next steps.
  • Turning the area director's bullet points into a polished newsletter or update.
  • Maintaining contact lists for leaders, parents and committee members.
  • Helping with expense receipts and reimbursements.
  • Helping send donor thank-you notes.

Basic Strategies to Develop

  • Start with a training session. Meet with the area director once to learn the calendar, key systems, recurring reports and communication tools. You will be given access to the Young Life systems you need.
  • Set a regular check-in. A short weekly or biweekly call or meeting with the area director keeps priorities clear and makes your hours more effective. Occasional in-person time helps too.
  • Build a simple rhythm. Keep a running list of recurring tasks and deadlines (weekly, monthly, seasonal) so the work becomes routine and easy to stay ahead of.
  • Grow the role over time. As you get comfortable, suggest one or two new areas where you could help, and agree together on what fits your time.

Qualifications

  • Excellent organizational skills; enjoys keeping things on track.
  • Possesses the gift of encouragement.
  • Cheerful, positive and easy to work with.
  • Comfortable serving behind the scenes.
  • Learns quickly and is comfortable with email, calendars and online tools.
  • Takes initiative and spots needs before being asked.
  • Trustworthy with confidential information.
